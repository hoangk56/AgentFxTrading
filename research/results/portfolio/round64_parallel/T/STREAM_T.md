# Stream T: perp delisting convergence on Binance USDT-M. No LIVE or DEMO candidate

Written Sun Nov 1 09:41:39 UTC 2026 (from `date -u`). Hypotheses declared at 09:07:59 UTC, before any 1m price was

## Answer

**None, for every cell.** There are two reasons, and either one is enough:

1. **Structural: IS 2020-21 has one usable event** (BTCST, 2021-03-12). The declared gate was >= 5 IS trades per
   cell. No cell can be graded at IS.
2. **On VAL the wedge the rule needs does not exist, and the declared rich side loses.** At T - 24 h the perp sits
   on spot: median gap +5.8 bp, IQR -12.5..+14.7 bp, n 30. Zero events had a perp > 50 bp rich, so T1 k50 / k100
   have n = 0. T1 k0 (short perp / long spot whenever the perp is rich at all) lost **-141.0 bp per trade, t -2.15,
   n 16, 1 / 16 wins**. It is still negative with the settlement-proxy exit (-83.3 bp) and at T - 6 h / T - 48 h.
   It also does worse than random entries on the same coins (beats 1.1 % of random). The perp **rises** relative
   to Binance spot into settlement, so the short-perp leg loses about 98 bp before costs.

T2 (long perp / short BTC) is a bet on the coin's own price. k0: -515.7 bp, t -0.35. The k50 (n 3) and k100 (n 1)
cells hold only FTX-week emergency events (SRM, +11,017 bp). Their announcement leads are unverified and probably
under 24 h, so they cannot be traded without look-ahead. There is no power: LIVE would need a per-trade Sharpe of at
least 0.96-2.2 at the HOLD sizes these rates imply (2-10 trades). The measured values are -0.56 to +0.13.

Of the 31 graded events, **9 have a verified lead >= 24 h** (DGB, CTK, SNT, STPT, RAD, CVX, UNFI, KEY, REN). The
others in the verified row lacked spot at T. Two graded events had leads < 24 h, and 20 are unverified. The ordinary
2024 notices gave 4-11 days. The emergency cases (LUNA / ANC 2022-05, FTT / SRM / RAY 2022-11) are the ones with
short or unknown leads. They are also the only large dislocations (SRM -1,153 bp at T - 24 h). **Remaining bias:**
the unverified emergency events could be look-ahead. They drive every positive T2 number, which is why T2 k50 / k100
cannot be believed. T1 k0 does not depend on them: ex-emergency it is identical (-141.0 bp, n 16). On the verified
subset it is -55.5 bp, median -128.2, n 7.

Settlement rule: Binance notices say "no new positions from T - 30 min" and close every position by automatic
settlement at T. The exact settlement-price formula was not found in public text. KuCoin and other venues use a
30-minute average index. Binance's index is a multi-venue composite. So the declared exit is the perp's last 1m
close (closing by hand is allowed until T). The settlement proxy (30-min mean of Binance spot) is a sensitivity
check. The two differ a lot on several events: UNFI +343 bp, MDT +218, REN +188, CTK +163, AUDIO +138, between the
perp's last print and Binance spot.

## Costs and power

- Costs as declared: T1 70 bp (perp 5 x 2, spot 10 x 2, 4 x 10 bp half-spread), T2 42 bp. The measured entry jump
  |open(E) - close(E - 1m)| was median ~2 bp on the perp, inside the 10 bp proxy, so costs are not what kills the
  rule. T1's gross is already -91 bp.
- Power at VAL size (HOLD trades estimated from the VAL rate x 1.83 yr): T1 k0 SR -0.56 with ~10 HOLD trades (needs
  >= 0.96). T2 k0 SR -0.11 with ~8 (needs >= 1.07). T2 k50 SR 0.13 with ~2 (needs >= 2.2). Even a true per-trade
  Sharpe of 0.5-0.7 (B's level) gives power ~2 with 10-20 HOLD events, so this family is DEMO-at-best by
  construction.
- Correlation with C3 is not computed: no cell reached DEMO numbers.

## Pitfalls found

- Local perp_1d (fapi) **drops the pre-relisting history** of perps that were delisted and later relisted (CVX,
  CTK, MAVIA, CVC, MDT, SLP, BTCST, YFII have no pre-2025 rows). Any survivorship-sensitive study built on perp_1d
  (round 60 listings, round 61 universes) misses these coins in their first life. data.binance.vision keeps them.
- After a settlement, both fapi and data.binance.vision keep **flat zero-trade klines** (many end 2024-05-28). Use
  the last bar with trades, not the last row.
- For a 09:00 settlement, the 09:00 1m bar often carries one final print (T shows as 09:01). It is not the Binance
  spot price: it differs by up to +343 bp (UNFI).
- Spot of the delisted coin is often already gone from Binance at the perp's settlement (ANT, LOOM, ORBS, XEM,
  MAVIA, BOND, HNT). A long-spot hedge then has to be on another venue.

## What a user decision could unlock

Little. Even with all announcement times (a paid timestamped dataset, or allowing /bapi/), IS 2020-21 has 1 event,
VAL has ~30, and the T - 24 h gap is ~0. A forward paper log of delisting notices could test an entry at the notice
(+5 min) rather than at T - 24 h. That is round 63 D's announcement-reaction family, not a convergence wedge, and it
would need the era split D proposed. I would not prioritise it.
