### C3 — Gotobi JPY basket (the recommended algorithm)

**Mechanism.** Japanese importers settle on *gotobi* days (the 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th, 25th and month-end) and buy
USD at the Tokyo 9:55 fixing; the yen weakens into the fix and gives it back afterwards (Ito & Yamada 2017;
the old lab's N2). The rule is N2 unchanged, applied to the three JPY pairs where it shows.

**Rules (one bot, 3 symbols, cap 3):**
- Days: 5th/10th/15th/20th/25th/last day of the month; a Saturday/Sunday moves to the Friday before.
- 18:00 UTC the evening before (03:00 Tokyo; Tokyo has no DST), or the first tick after the weekend open for a
  Monday: **buy** USDJPY, EURJPY, GBPJPY.
- 00:55 UTC (09:55 Tokyo, the fix): close the longs, **sell** all three.
- 02:55 UTC (11:55 Tokyo): close the shorts.
- Stop 3 x ATR20(H1) from the fill; lot = default $ risk / stop distance (1x default; volatility sets the lot,
  capital never does). Max hold 7 h, never more than 3 positions (close before open at 00:55).

**Evidence.**
- Retest of the *unchanged* old-lab code on 2016-2022 — years that did not exist when N2 was designed on
  2023-2026: USDJPY R +0.078, t 5.7, 7/7 years positive, beats 10/10 random-entry seeds (pre-registered bar,
  passed). With the old lab's own 2023-24 (+0.041) and 2025-26 (+0.035), USDJPY is positive in every year
  2016-2026. At t 5.7 this survives a Bonferroni correction over every configuration in v1 + v2 (~350).
- Ported to `research/scan/swing.py` with historical swaps and risk-scaled lots: USDJPY IS +0.066 / VAL +0.054 /
  HOLD +0.038. EURJPY (IS +0.037, VAL +0.040) and GBPJPY (IS +0.027, VAL +0.029) were added on IS/VAL only and
  registered before their HOLD: +0.057 and +0.031.
- **Placebo:** the same hours on non-gotobi weekdays: IS -0.012, VAL +0.002 — the effect is the settlement flow.
- **Timing is the fix.** Long legs ending at 00:55 UTC: IS +0.055 (t 4.1); ending 10 min later (01:05): +0.017;
  at 01:55: +0.006. Short legs starting at 00:55: IS +0.031 (t 3.6); starting at 01:05: -0.020 (t -2.6). The long
  leg's start (17:00..21:00 UTC) hardly matters. That is what a fixing flow looks like, not a curve fit.
- Costs: spread at the fix is normal (USDJPY 0.13 bp, 100+ ticks/min); 1.5x spread keeps every era positive
  (HOLD +0.019R). Swaps from historical rates: the 2026-constant swaps of the old lab overstated USDJPY's IS by
  0.008R (0.074 vs 0.066).
- Every year 2016-2026 net positive: 2016 +$3,035 · 2017 +$2,048 · 2018 +$1,126 · 2019 +$1,717 · 2020 +$1,028 ·
  2021 +$2,127 · 2022 +$2,257 · 2023 +$895 · 2024 +$2,103 · 2025 +$1,534 · 2026 (to Sep) +$1,581.
  Total +$19,450 on $100 risk per trade (4,590 trades, ~420/yr, 56 % winners, longest losing run 16), max
  drawdown $1,261 (12.6 R), monthly Sharpe 1.68.

**Caveats.** Japanese public holidays are not modelled (no fix those days; the bot should skip them — expected
~0 effect, so this only adds noise). The effect is small per trade (~0.04 R); it earns through frequency, so
spread discipline matters (do not trade it on a wide-spread account). USDJPY's own HOLD t is 1.3 on its own: the
basket is what reaches t 2.9.

### C1 — Crypto Donchian breakout, H4 (promising, decaying)

**Rules (one bot per symbol, BTCUSD and ETHUSD):** on an H4 close above the prior 55-bar high buy (below the
55-bar low sell); every qualifying bar is an entry, so a persistent breakout stacks up to 3 units; exit on an H4
close below the prior 20-bar low (shorts: above the 20-bar high), the 3 x ATR20(H4) stop, or 5 days. Lot = default
risk x clip(breakout distance in ATR / causal median of earlier breakouts, 0.5, 2).

**Evidence for:** all 12 Donchian configs tried on crypto were positive in IS and VAL (a plateau, not a pick);
delaying entries 1/2/6 bars decays the edge gradually (+0.26/+0.18/+0.12 R IS) instead of breaking it; random
same-side entries with the same exits lose to it 20/20 seeds in IS and VAL; it is the one trend rule that works
nowhere but crypto (indices, metals, energies, FX all negative), consistent with crypto's documented time-series
momentum. The breakout-strength lot multiplier was built into the framework before crypto was tested, and strong
breakouts beat weak ones in every era (IS +0.40 vs +0.15 R, VAL +0.29 vs +0.16, HOLD +0.14 vs -0.19).

**Evidence against:** HOLD mean R -0.025 (BTCUSD -0.151, ETHUSD +0.123), so it failed the registered bar (net $ > 0
*and* mean R > 0) even though it made +$2,859 there thanks to the lot multiplier. By year, R went +0.38, +0.24,
+0.42, +0.08, +0.16, +0.36, +0.19 (2018-2024) and then 0.00, -0.06 (2025, 2026): a crypto market with ETFs and
institutional flow may simply trend less at this horizon. Total 2018-2026 +$40,311, max DD $3,578.

**Use:** only as a separate, small bot with a kill switch (e.g. stop when the rolling 100-trade mean R < 0);
monthly P&L correlation with C3 is -0.10.

## Method (what changed from v1)

- **Engine** `research/scan/swing.py`: per-bot cap (up to 3, same-symbol stacking allowed), exits on stop, target,
  a strategy's own exit signal or 5 days; D1 bars close at 20:00 UTC (before the rollover spread), Friday stubs
  merged; entries/exits filled at the next M1 open with the real bid/ask.
- **Swaps** `research/scan/rates.py`: FRED short rates (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, NZD; HKD = USD) +
  per-symbol markup fitted to IC's 2026 swaps. research/specs.SWAPS (USD per unit, 2026) overstated early-year
  index swaps by the price ratio and ignored 2016-21's near-zero rates.
- **Three eras** IS 2016-21 / VAL 2022-24 / HOLD 2025-01..2026-09, HOLD opened once per candidate after
  pre-registration (`prereg_swing_v2.md`, with sha256 of the code). Literature rules were run with published
  parameters, so for them all of 2016-2026 is out of sample.
- **Old-lab retest**: `python -m research.lab.run --years 2016..2022` on the unchanged catalog code
  (`research/results/lab/retest_2016_2022*`), verdict by `python -m research.scan.retest_verdict RUN_DIR`.
