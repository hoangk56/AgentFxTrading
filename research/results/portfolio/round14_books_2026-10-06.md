## Q. A book of stable clock effects
- Rule (written 01:48 UTC, PRE and IS columns of round 13 M's cells only): pools of the top-500 PRE cells or all
  cells with |t_PRE| >= 3.5 (1,644); side from PRE; IS side t >= 2, IS net > 0, IS net t >= 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0; greedy by
  IS net t with no same-symbol overlap and |rho| <= 0.5 between legs; equal risk or 0.5-2x by IS Sharpe. 12 books.
- Results (per day, day-clustered t; power on the VAL size):

| book | legs | IS day net (t) | VAL day net (t) | VAL/IS | power IS / VAL | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| primary (top-500 pool, t >= 1.5, equal risk) | E1 + gold before UK holidays | +1.24 (3.5) | +1.35 (2.5) | 1.09 | 1.90 / 1.89 | it is E1 (96-98 % of the risk); not registered |
| widest (1,644 pool, t >= 1.0, Sharpe-weighted) | 9 | +4.21 (5.5) | +1.56 (1.4) | 0.37 | 2.98 / 1.05 | fails VAL t and power |
| best (1,644 pool, t >= 2.0, Sharpe-weighted) | 3: EURUSD short 09:00-13:00 London, EURJPY short at quarter-end, GBPUSD long at month-end | +1.47 (4.7) | +1.30 (2.6) | 0.88 | 2.55 / 2.01 | fails power |

- The legs are nearly uncorrelated (mean rho +0.02; effective bets 8.2 of 9). If every leg had kept its IS Sharpe,
  the 9-leg book would have reached Sharpe 0.158-0.170 per day (power 3.3-3.6): LIVE. The real IS book is 0.141
  (correlation costs ~10 %), but the VAL book is 0.050: **5 of 9 legs keep under 20 % of their IS net in VAL.** A
  30-65 % drop in the mid of a leg turns its 0.7-0.9 bp net into ~0 after costs. Without E1 the other legs make IS
  +3.11 (t 3.67) and VAL -0.09 (t -0.07).
- Possible lead (3 events a year, DEMO-sized at most): EURJPY short 05:00-07:00 London on the last weekday of the
  quarter, mid +10.7 / +8.7 / +9.6 bp in PRE / IS / VAL.

## R. Conditions for the stable clock effects
- 96 counted cells: 5 objects (E1, the ECB pre-fix slot 12:45-13:00 London, USDJPY midday, USDCHF midday, GBPJPY New
  York afternoon) x selectors (the pair's morning move, yesterday's window, the 20-day trailing effect, the overnight
  USD move, US500's morning) x exclusions (ECB days, home holidays, German-only holidays, the weeks when London-New
  York is 4 h). 20 cells pass the gate (uplift > 0 in every era, net > 0 in IS and VAL); none reaches LIVE.
- Best selectors:

| cell | trades / yr | PRE / IS / VAL mid | net IS (t) / VAL (t) | power IS / VAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 on days with a top-tercile overnight USD rise (USDJPY, USDCHF) | 84 | +1.84 / +3.10 / +3.05 | +2.47 (3.81) / +2.43 (2.41) | 2.05 / 1.83 |
| E1 when the euro fell 07:00-11:00 London | 128 | +2.06 / +2.25 / +2.31 | +1.62 (3.24) / +1.69 (2.15) | 1.74 / 1.63 |
| ECB pre-fix slot when its 20-day trailing mean > 0 | 188 | +0.65 / +1.30 / +1.58 | +0.64 (2.99) / +0.95 (3.50) | 1.60 / 2.66 (PRE net < 0) |
| USDCHF midday after a top-tercile USDCHF morning rise | 82 | +2.27 / +1.97 / +1.58 | +1.04 (1.67) / +0.71 (0.82) | 0.90 / 0.62 |

- The selectors scale E1 by 1.5-1.7x in IS/VAL but only 1.1x in PRE, and they cut the trade count about in half or
  more, so power does not move (lot sizing 2x / 0.5x instead of filtering: per-day power 2.14 / 2.02). As a family
  they are not beyond chance (best IS uplift t 3.16 against a permutation 95th percentile of 3.31).
- **Mechanism check:** on TARGET2 closing days (Good Friday, Easter Monday, 1 May; no ECB reference rate) E1 reverses,
  -3.9 bp against +1.8 on other days (2004-24, block-clustered t -2.7, 35 blocks). This supports the ECB-fix link.
  If E1 ever runs on demo, skipping those days is justified by the mechanism alone.
- Post hoc: E1 was larger on ECB decision days through 2021 (+4.0, t 2.4), gone in VAL (decisions moved to 14:15 CET);
  larger on German-only holidays (+5.2, t 4.0, but ~0 in IS); the "placebo" holidays of other countries are not null.

## What this says
- **The book idea works in theory and fails on decay.** Stable, nearly independent legs exist, and nine of them
  would clear LIVE if they kept their IS size. Most lose 35-80 % of their mid from IS to VAL, and IC's 0.6-1 bp cost
  turns the rest into zero.
- **Selectors trade count for edge one for one.** Doubling E1's edge on a third of the days leaves its power at ~2.
- **E1 is the best structural FX effect this data has**, and it is about half of LIVE. With the current symbols and
  costs, LIVE needs a new effect of a different size, not a better cut of the known ones.
