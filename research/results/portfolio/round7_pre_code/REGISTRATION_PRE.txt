AMENDMENT 1 (2026-10-04 14:27 UTC, still before any PRE strategy number was computed). Reason: a data-quality look
at raw Dukascopy files (no strategy computed) shows the early ask side is not a usable cost: USDJPY 2004-01..08 has
bid == ask in 60-100 % of minutes (median spread 0), USDJPY 2005 has a 2-3 pip quoted spread, GBPJPY 2004-05 a
constant 4.0 pips at every hour. Engine fills on that ask would make costs era-specific artefacts. Therefore:
 PRIMARY cost (judged): Dukascopy BID prices (validated vs IC) + a constant synthetic spread per pair = IC's mean
   2016-2017 spread at the minutes before the strategy's fills (ca = c + const); + 0.6 bp commission + swaps
   (FRED - 1 %). C3 (17:59/00:54/02:54): USDJPY 0.34, EURJPY 0.66, GBPJPY 1.46 pips. M1 (09:59/12:59): USDJPY
   0.28, USDCHF 0.55, EURUSD 0.09 pips. Spread x1.5 = 1.5 x these constants.
 SECONDARY (reported, not judged): Dukascopy's own ask; GROSS = zero spread, zero commission, no swap.
Bars and everything else unchanged.

AMENDMENT 3 (2026-10-04 15:22 UTC, before any PRE strategy number). The 2016-2017 engine check (IS data) showed the
constant spread of amendment 1 is optimistic vs IC's real spreads (Duka bid + const vs IC raw: R +0.006 for
USDJPY+EURJPY, +0.010 GBPJPY), because Monday-gotobi long legs enter ~5 min after the weekly open, where IC's
spread is 3 (USDJPY) to 15 (GBPJPY) pips. PRIMARY cost is changed to an IC 2016-2017 spread PROFILE: ask = bid +
IC's mean spread at the same UTC minute-of-day, or, for the first 60 min after a gap >= 60 min (weekly open,
holiday reopen), IC's mean spread at the same minute after the reopen (run_pre.spread_profile). Check: IC bid +
profile vs IC raw R diff +0.0002 (USDJPY+EURJPY) / -0.0013 (GBPJPY); Duka bid + profile vs IC raw +0.0013 /
+0.0030. Spread x1.5 = 1.5 x the profile. The constant spread becomes a secondary line. Same for M1.
Everything else (rules, bars, commission, swaps) unchanged.
